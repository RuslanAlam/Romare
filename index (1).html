Our Story

The Story Behind ROMARE

I. Beginnings

Growing up, I always had a passion for design. It began with cars. I loved sketching different wheel designs, headlights, body shapes, and imagining how existing designs could be improved.

Eventually, that curiosity expanded into architecture. I spent countless hours designing homes on paper, through digital applications, and even building them virtually in Fortnite.

II. Family and Mentors

As I grew older, I became increasingly involved in academics, business, and other responsibilities. Coming from a fourth-generation business family, I grew up with the expectation of eventually contributing to and expanding our family's business.

My father has always been one of my greatest mentors. Beyond business, he taught me the importance of manners, character, discipline, and presenting myself well.

I developed a deep appreciation for classic menswear: well-fitted shirts, ties, suits, polished shoes, watches, and the small details that create a distinguished appearance.

One saying has remained particularly meaningful to me:

“Manners maketh man.”

William of Wykeham

III. Dressing Well

To me, dressing well is not simply about appearing wealthy or wearing expensive clothes. It is about confidence, self-respect, and how we carry ourselves.

I also find great satisfaction in meeting people, exchanging ideas, teaching, advising, and helping others. I hope that through my work, I can encourage men to feel confident in how they present themselves, while remembering that character and manners are what truly define a gentleman.

IV. The First Design

Although my long-term ambitions remain connected to entrepreneurship, global trade, and the maritime industry, my love for design has never disappeared.

I had always imagined creating my own menswear brand. For years, it remained a dream.

When I began working on a product-design project through my engineering studies, I finally had an opportunity to explore that dream.

I started by thinking about something I enjoyed wearing myself: quarter-zips paired with shirts and ties. During colder weather, I would often add a cardigan or jacket over them.

I wondered whether I could combine the appearance and practicality of these garments into one.

That idea led to my first integrated quarter-zip cardigan concept.

ROMARE is the beginning of my journey to bring together creativity, simplicity, and the art of dressing well.

Ruslan Alam

The Design

Two Classics.
One Garment.

A wool cardigan outside and a knitted quarter-zip within, permanently joined as one garment. Nothing detaches.

Front, back, side and open-inside views

Construction

Outer Structure

A smooth wool-blend cardigan covering the shoulders, sleeves, back, and outer front.

Inner Structure

A permanently attached rib-knit quarter-zip chest panel, with a short zipper running only from the neck to the upper chest.

Buttons

Three visible front buttons. The upper two form the main closure, and the lowest sits near the hem.

Pockets

Two exterior pockets, with discreet storage pockets inside.

Branding

A small gold-and-navy embroidered mare-head emblem on the left chest. The interior label reads:

ROMARERUSLAN ALAM
